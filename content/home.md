## Willkommen! 👋 Welcome to your German-for-IT journey

This handbook has **one job**: take you from **B1** to **B2/C1** and into a **software
engineering job in Germany** — in about **12 months** of focused, daily practice.

Everything here is built around *your* profile: a **backend Java / Spring Boot developer**
who already thinks in microservices, Docker, Kubernetes and REST — you just need the
**German** to do it all *auf Deutsch*: dailies, code reviews, architecture discussions,
emails, and interviews.

### How to use this app

1. **Follow the 6 phases in order.** Each phase builds on the last. Start with
   [Roadmap Overview](#/roadmap-overview), then open [Phase 1 · Objectives](#/phase-1/overview).
2. **Do a little every day.** Consistency beats marathons. Each phase has a **daily plan**
   (~2–3 h/day: 30 min morning, 30 min afternoon, 1–2 h evening).
3. **Mark modules complete** (button at the bottom of each lesson) to track progress — see
   the bar in the sidebar.
4. **Drill vocabulary** in [Flashcards](#/@flashcards) and test yourself with
   [Quizzes](#/@quiz). Tick off your week in the [Weekly Checklist](#/@checklist).
5. **Speak out loud.** Use the 🔊 buttons (German text-to-speech) to hear pronunciation,
   then shadow it. Speaking is a *motor skill* — reps matter more than theory.
6. **Bookmark** (☆) anything you want to revisit, and use **Search** (`/`) to jump anywhere.

### The language of this handbook

Explanations are in **English**; every example is in **German** with an English gloss.
Vietnamese appears only for the *trickiest* words (`VI:` notes). This mirrors how you'll
actually work: think in English/Vietnamese today, operate in German tomorrow.

### A promise and a warning

> **Promise:** if you complete the phases and speak daily, B2 in ~6–8 months and C1-level
> job readiness in ~12 months is realistic for a motivated engineer.
>
> **Warning:** passive watching ≠ learning. Output (speaking + writing) is where fluency is
> built. This app pushes you to produce, not just consume.
